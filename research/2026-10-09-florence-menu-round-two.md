# Florence restaurant menu research — 2026-10-09

## Osteria Zio Gigi

[TheFork Italian menu](https://www.thefork.it/ristorante/osteria-zio-gigi-r853218/menu), updated 27 March 2026, lists:
- Pappardelle al cinghiale
- Bistecca alla fiorentina (min 500g) (per 100g)
- Ribollita toscana o minestrone
- Spezzatino di manzo peposo alla fiorentina
- Trippa alla fiorentina

All are **reported** third-party menu items, not confirmed restaurant-owned menus. Note the exact prep of the peposo differs from the canonical name.

## Trattoria Sergio Gozzi

[Florence Made in Tuscany editorial profile](https://www.firenzemadeintuscany.com/it/place/trattoria-sergio-gozzi/) reports peposo, bistecca alla fiorentina, trippa, summer pappa al pomodoro, and winter ribollita. This is a rotating daily menu; none should be treated as guaranteed for a particular date. 'Trippa' is not necessarily the exact 'Trippa alla fiorentina' preparation.
