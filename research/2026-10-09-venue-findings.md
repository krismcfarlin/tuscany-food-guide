## Most useful finding

**Mickey's Cecineria is already one of the original Pisa stops** and the owner-managed listing specifically describes serving classic **5e5** (cecina in bread). Its [delivery menu](https://www.justeat.it/restaurants-mickeys-cecineria-pisa/menu) also lists **Focaccia con Cecina**. We have reported evidence for this dish, not a restaurant-owned current menu confirmation.

**L'Antico Trippaio** explicitly lists **Lampredotto** and **Trippa alla fiorentina** on its official site.

**Panimo** has a restaurant-owned menu showing **Schiacciata Toscana**. Do not infer that it serves cecina.

**Osteria La Dolce Vita** has third-party menu evidence for **Bistecca alla fiorentina**, but the published menu was last updated in November 2025.
